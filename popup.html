    Stay Scam-Safe

    Key rules for job hunting in India

    🚨 Never do these
    💸
    Never pay to get a job

    Any request for registration fee, security deposit, or training fee is a scam. Legitimate employers never charge candidates.

    🪪
    Never share Aadhaar or PAN before being hired

    Real employers only need ID documents after a formal offer letter, not during the application stage.

    🏦
    Never share bank details to "receive training materials"

    This is the oldest trick. No employer needs your account number before hiring you.

    ⚡ Be cautious when
    📱
    Contact is WhatsApp-only

    Legitimate companies use professional email. WhatsApp-only recruiters are a common scam vector.

    🎯
    Salary seems too high for the role

    Check market rates on Glassdoor. If a fresher role offers ₹80,000/month, something is wrong.

    ⏱️
    Immediate job offer without interview

    Real companies have a selection process. Instant offers are pressure tactics.

    ✅ Always verify
    🌐
    Check company on MCA21

    Search the company name on MCA21 portal to confirm they are registered in India.

    🔎
    Search "[Company] + scam + reviews"

    Do a quick Google search before applying. Real scam companies have warning posts by past victims.

    📧
    Verify recruiter email domain

    If the company is "TechCorp" but the email is @gmail.com, that's a red flag. Official companies use @techcorp.com.
